## Notes on Source Selection

This bibliography represents the major scholarly, investigative, and reference works on the kidney thieves urban legend and related topics. Sources were selected based on:

1. **Academic Authority:** Leading folklore scholars and researchers
2. **Medical Credibility:** Professional medical organizations and peer-reviewed journals
3. **Investigative Rigor:** Thorough journalism and fact-checking
4. **Theoretical Depth:** Cultural and social theory applications
5. **Real-World Context:** Actual organ trafficking research for comparison
6. **Historical Range:** Coverage from legend emergence to present
7. **International Scope:** Global perspectives and variations

The sources distinguish clearly between the urban legend (thoroughly debunked) and real organ trafficking (documented but operating differently), providing comprehensive context for understanding both the folklore phenomenon and the legitimate medical ethics issues.
